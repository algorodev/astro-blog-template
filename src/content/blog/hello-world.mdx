Welcome to your new Astro blog! This is a sample post to get you started.

## What you get

- **Content Collections** — type-safe frontmatter validation with Zod
- **MDX** — use JSX components directly inside your Markdown posts
- **Tailwind CSS** — utility-first styling with the typography plugin for prose
- **RSS feed** — available at `/rss.xml`
- **Sitemap** — auto-generated at `/sitemap-index.xml`
- **Tag pages** — posts are filterable by tag at `/blog/tags/[tag]`
- **Draft support** — set `draft: true` in frontmatter to hide a post from production

## Writing posts

Create a new `.mdx` file inside `src/content/blog/` with the required frontmatter fields:

```md
---
title: My Post Title
description: A short description for SEO and post cards.
pubDate: 2024-06-01
tags: [tag1, tag2]
draft: false
---

Your content here...
```

## Next steps

- Update `astro.config.mjs` with your real `site` URL
- Edit `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` to change the site name and navigation
- Replace this sample post with your own writing
- Customize `tailwind.config.mjs` with your brand colors and fonts
